# 🧪 DiluteIt | Host Cell Lab Suite
> **Universal dilution solver. Precision guaranteed.**

DiluteIt is the third installment of the **Host Cell** laboratory suite, designed by **Emiliano Balderas** (IBt-UNAM). It focuses on the most frequent task in biotechnology: calculating dilutions with different units of concentration and volume.

## 🧬 Scientific Fundamentals

The application is governed by the Law of Conservation of Mass for solutions:

$$C_1 V_1 = C_2 V_2$$

Where:
* **$C_1$**: Concentration of the stock solution.
* **$V_1$**: Volume of the stock needed (Inoculum/Aliquot).
* **$C_2$**: Final desired concentration.
* **$V_2$**: Final total volume of the solution.

## ⚡ Key Features
* **Unit Agnostic:** Mix Molar, mM, and µM or percentages seamlessly.
* **Universal Solver:** Choose any of the 4 variables as the unknown.
* **Mobile-First:** High-contrast, large touch targets for use with lab gloves.
* **Offline Ready:** PWA architecture for windowless lab environments.
